6. AI-Assisted Authoring

The Software includes AI-assisted authoring features that invoke a large language model to draft prose content within certification documents. You acknowledge and agree that:

The Software constrains the AI to operate only over your project data and forbids the AI from fabricating safety-critical claims (failure conditions, severity classifications, Development Assurance Level allocations, quantitative reliability values, regulatory citations) not present in your project data.

AI output is advisory and non-deterministic, and does not compute or alter the deterministic quantitative safety results produced by the Software.

Notwithstanding such constraints, large language models can produce inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate output, and the AI's output requires your review and acceptance before it is incorporated into any document.

Human verification by qualified personnel is required before final approval of all AI-generated artifacts: no AI-assisted output, including any analysis, computed or suggested value, severity classification, Development Assurance Level, safety requirement, fault-tree structure, or report, may be finalized, approved, released, or relied upon for any engineering, safety, or certification purpose until a qualified human engineer has independently reviewed, verified, and accepted it and assumed professional responsibility for it.

The Software's user interface enforces a per-section human disposition gate, requiring you to explicitly Accept, Edit, or Discard each AI-generated section before document finalization. The disposition gate is a feature of the Software, but the responsibility for the accuracy of dispositioned content is yours.

Licensor disclaims any warranty regarding the accuracy of AI-generated content. The AI output is provided as a drafting aid, not as a substitute for engineering judgment.

Where AI requests are routed depends on your Deployment Model. In a Hosted Deployment they are routed through a proxy operated by Licensor to identified language-model providers on a per-request basis. In a Customer-Hosted Deployment or a Local-Only Deployment they are routed through an inference proxy you operate, using provider credentials you hold, and they do not reach Licensor.
