Guides

  • Fault Tree Analysis software: building and quantifying trees, repeated events, dynamic gates, importance measures and DAL allocation.
  • The ARP 4761A safety assessment process: FHA, PASA, PSSA, SSA and the supporting analyses, end to end, with full traceability.
  • ARP 4754B & DAL allocation: top-down development assurance, the §5.4 options, FDAL and IDAL.
  • FMEA software: functional and piece-part FMEA wired into the fault tree and component library.
  • CMA, PRA & ZSA: the common-cause analyses, connected to the quantification they should influence.

Safety Lab Aero is built by an aerospace engineer for the people who actually have to certify aircraft. Everything above lives in one connected model: change something in one analysis and the tool shows you what it affects everywhere else.
