The golden thread · live

“Integrated” isn’t a layout. It’s one connected thread.

The promise at the top of this page is a single model, not a bundle of tools. One aircraft function flows down to its systems, its STPA hazards, a failure condition, a fault tree, the common-cause analyses (PRA, ZSA, CMA) that test its independence, the safety requirements that control it, and the verification that closes each one. Reliability and human factors run beneath as feeder lanes, wired into the exact artifact they inform rather than parked in a separate tool. That unbroken line is the golden thread, and it’s what “integrated” actually buys you: move the design and the whole thread moves with it, because there is only one source of truth to drift from.

Human Factors

The crew is a component. Model it like one.

Most safety cases credit the pilot in prose (“crew detects and reverts”) and never say how long that takes, whether anyone measured it, or what else the crew was doing at the time. Safety Lab makes the human claim a typed, cited, checkable record on the same golden thread as the fault trees, so an unearned credit fails an invariant instead of surviving review.

📖

298 page-cited reference values

Reaction times, detection thresholds, reach and workload data drawn from the NASA Human Integration Design Handbook, each carrying its section, author-year, the printed page, and a verbatim quote containing the number. Every value is tagged aircraft, spaceflight, or general, because HIDH is a spaceflight handbook and pretending otherwise is how a bad number gets credited. A preset seeds a field; it never fills one, and never marks anything validated.

🔒

Two lanes: credited and uncredited

Every human-factors assumption can carry both the value you hope to credit and the conservative value you must use until someone proves it. The credited lane applies only once the assumption is Validated or Verified. A Catastrophic or Hazardous claim resting on an unvalidated credit is a hard invariant failure, not a comment or a warning.

⏱

Workload saturation, arithmetic not vibes

Per crewmember, per flight phase, across the five HIDH channels (visual, auditory, cognitive, psychomotor, verbal), declared co-active tasks are summed against the 80% time-occupancy red line. Ledger arithmetic: no solver, no pilot model, no simulation. And an exposure duration is never silently reused as a response window; if the phase declares no window, the check declines to run rather than invent one.

⚖️

Severity reconciled against workload

AC 25.1309 writes severity in workload language, so the two can be compared. A task the model says drives excessive workload sitting under a Minor severity classification is a two-band gap, and the FHA row says so on its face. Advisory only: it flags the contradiction for an engineer to resolve, and never overrides your classification.

🧪

Trials enter as evidence, not as truth

Sim campaigns, flight test, bench trials and literature ingest with metric, value, sample size and a real source, because uncited evidence is rumor, so the source field is mandatory. Measured worse than assumed reads CONTRADICTS; at or better reads SUPPORTS. Nothing self-validates: a human validates in the register, and a Validated assumption whose own evidence contradicts it gets flagged until someone answers for it.

📏

Ergonomics on the standards spine

ISO 9241 and MIL-STD-1472 sit on the spine by designation, title and role: cite-and-point, never a word of licensed text stored. A Fitts’ Law movement-time calculator in the Shannon formulation that requires cited coefficients, because a borrowed coefficient without a citation is a guess wearing a suit. Seven-factor human-performance taxonomy for STPA causal factors, with NASA-HFACS nanocodes as the deep layer.

Every hazard call, severity, independence claim, β, and acceptance carries your signature. The engine derives everything else from those decisions and re-derives it when they change. Computed output never overwrites an engineer’s word.

Your assumptions, load-bearing

Every claim knows what it rests on. Assumptions bind to the analyses, model rules, dispatch limits and dispositions built on them. Invalidate one, and every exposed claim is named on the spot. An assumption nothing rests on gets flagged too.

The discipline

What the tool refuses to do.

Anyone can add features. The reason a DER trusts a workspace is what it will not let slide. These guardrails are not a mode you switch on. They are the design.

🧮

The AI never does the math

The deterministic engine owns every computation. The AI drafts structure and accelerates the busywork, but it never does the math, and nothing it produces enters your analysis until a qualified engineer reviews and accepts it.

✍️

Nothing is approved on trust

An approval is a re-authenticated signature bound to the exact analysis it signs. If the analysis changes after sign-off, the signature no longer matches that state, and the record says so.

⛓️

The record can’t be quietly changed

Saves, sealed revisions, and sign-offs land in an append-only, hash-chained ledger. Any later edit or deletion of a past record is mathematically detectable: the audit trail a certification authority expects.

🔍

The math is never a black box

Every engine is independently verified against textbook benchmarks, the references are cited, and any computation traces back to the standard it implements. You can always check our work.
